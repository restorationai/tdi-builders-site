TDI Builders is a Sacramento remodeling contractor that has been improving homes across Northern California since 1985. Kitchens, bathrooms, whole-home renovations and room additions are the heart of what we do, and every project is managed by one licensed and insured team from the first estimate to the final walkthrough.

## Remodeling services

**Kitchen Remodeling**, The kitchen is where a remodel pays off every day. TDI handles layout changes, cabinets, countertops, flooring and the trades behind the walls, coordinated as one project instead of a string of separate contractors.

**Bathroom Remodeling**, From a refreshed hall bath to a full primary suite, TDI plans the work around how your household actually uses the room, and keeps the schedule and the mess under control while the work is underway.

**Whole-Home Remodeling and Renovations**, Opening up a floor plan, updating an older home, or reworking several rooms at once takes a contractor who can sequence the trades. TDI manages the whole job so you are not left chasing subcontractors.

**Room Additions and Garages**, When the house no longer fits the family, an addition or a new garage adds space without a move. TDI builds additions that tie into the existing structure and match the home.

## Why homeowners trust TDI with their remodel

Forty years of building in the Sacramento Valley means TDI has worked on nearly every kind of home in the region: post-war tract homes in the Pocket and Meadowview, older Craftsman and Victorian houses in Curtis Park and Land Park, and newer neighborhoods out toward Elk Grove, Roseville and Folsom. Each one opens up differently once the walls come down, and that experience is what keeps a remodel on track when something unexpected turns up.

TDI is a proudly Native American owned company (Cherokee), licensed and insured since 1985. You get a written estimate before any work begins, a clear process from first visit to final sign-off, and one team accountable for the result.

## When a remodel starts with damage

Sometimes a remodel is not planned. When water, fire or storm damage forces part of a home back to the studs, TDI works with your insurance carrier, handles the claim paperwork, and rebuilds the space.
